FAQ

For schools & organizers.

Is the music school-appropriate?

Yes. We play radio/clean edits only and can steer clear of any songs or artists your school doesn't want played.

Can students send requests ahead of time?

Yes — organizers get a planning login where you can add must-play and do-not-play lists before the dance.

How far ahead should we book?

Homecoming and prom weekends book up early. Check your date and send a request as soon as your date is set.

How do we pay?

We send an invoice you can pay online, or by check.
